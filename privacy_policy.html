K-Viet Talk Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

K-Viet Talk is a Korean and Vietnamese learning app. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Information We Collect

K-Viet Talk does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Account and Login

The app does not require account registration or login. It does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other account details.

Learning Data

The app uses local JSON learning data included with the app package. This content is used only to display Korean and Vietnamese vocabulary, sentences, conversations, and quizzes.

Favorites

If you mark learning items as favorites, those preferences are saved only on your device using local app storage. Favorites are not sent to any server.

Permissions

K-Viet Talk does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use Firebase, advertising networks, analytics services, cloud servers, or user upload services.

Children's Privacy

K-Viet Talk does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Data Security

Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information, no personal information is stored on our servers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at cosana541@gmail.com.
